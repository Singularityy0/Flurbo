How settlement works

From trading close to payout, what the bot does by itself, which questions Flurbo can settle automatically, and when a person has to step in. Based on contracts/src/PilotResolver.sol, apps/web/server/resolution-worker.mjs and the settlement readers, as deployed on 1 October 2026.

Life of one question

StageWhenWho actsRules
TradingFrom listing until the pool's closeMembers and agentsOne close per pool, enforced on-chain. Nothing can be bought or sold after it.
CaptureEach question's capture window (opens after the close)BotThe first complete, valid reading inside the window is retained and decides. A 404, an empty list, a missing row or a null is not an answer: the bot keeps checking until the window closes.
AssertFrom the window close (the observation end) until observation end plus the assertion periodBot for auto pools; otherwise a person1 AUSD bond. The evidence (rule, raw capture, hashes) is archived publicly first and re-checked before signing.
ChallengeChallenge period after the assertion (12 hours for the wave 2 pools, 24 hours for Release milestones)Any wallet that is not a reviewer and not the asserterThe disputer posts the same bond with a different answer and evidence.
VoteVoting period after a disputeThe 3 reviewers (operator-controlled, disclosed)2 matching votes decide. If the panel agrees with one side, that side receives both bonds. If the panel picks a third answer, that answer stands and each bond is returned. With no quorum by the voting deadline, each bond is returned and the question is VOID.
FinalizeAfter the challenge or voting deadlineAnyone; the bot finalizes its own assertions and every timeoutAn unchallenged answer becomes final. The bot does not finalize assertions made by other wallets; whoever asserted by hand calls finalize. Bonds are not sent back automatically: they become a credit in the resolver that the owner collects with withdrawBond() (on the site: Collect bond credit). The bot never withdraws the settlement signer's credits.
DeliverWhen every question in the pool is finalAnyone; the bot does itSends the results to the pool. Payouts open.
RedeemAny time after deliveryEach memberMembers collect in Portfolio. Nothing expires.

What a share pays

ResultSingle Yes shareAND of two questions
Yes1 AUSD1 if both Yes
No00 if either is a known No
VOID0.5 AUSD (Yes and No each get half)0.5 if one is VOID and the other Yes; 0.25 if both VOID

VOID is not a refund of the purchase price. Payouts round down to the AUSD atom.

Bonds: an unchallenged asserter gets the bond back as a credit. After a vote, the side the panel agrees with is credited both bonds; a third answer or a voting timeout credits each side its own bond. Credits are collected with withdrawBond().

What the bot does each cycle

  1. Monitor. Reads every registered pool's on-chain state and emails alerts: assertion windows open, assertions to review, finalizations due, read failures. Pools far from their next action are re-read about every 8 minutes; delivered pools once per restart. It pings the Healthchecks.io watchdog.
  2. Collect sources. For each auto pool with a capture window open, reads the committed source URLs and stores complete readings with their hashes.
  3. Act. Puts every due action across all pools into one queue, earliest deadline first: assert, finalize, deliver. One signer, one pending transaction at a time, up to 12 transactions per cycle. Each transaction is journaled before it is sent; a restart resumes from the journal and never signs a replacement.
  4. Isolate failures. A pool whose source or chain read fails is skipped and reported. The others carry on.

Cadence: every 10 minutes, and every 2 minutes from 15 minutes before anything is due. A pool listed in FLURBO_RESOLUTION_HOLD keeps collecting evidence but the bot never asserts for it.

Not automatic

  • Sources behind a login or a secret or paid API key (nobody else could check the evidence).
  • Pages built only by JavaScript, PDFs and images.
  • Anything subjective or needing interpretation.
  • Out of scope by policy: sports, pop culture and awards.

Assert by hand

Use a wallet that is not a reviewer and is not the settlement signer (asserting from the signer would confuse the bot's journal), and never the old signer. The deployer works. Outcome codes: 1 = No, 2 = Yes, 3 = VOID. Questions are numbered from 0 in pool order.

RESOLVER=0xe72386686b03d7e04554505ea0ef00ffa04ec73e   # the pool's resolver (table below)
EVENT=1                                               # question index
SOURCE=https://api.github.com/repos/paradigmxyz/reth/releases?per_page=30

# 1. Save the evidence and hash it
curl -s "$SOURCE" -o evidence.json
HASH=$(cast keccak "$(cat evidence.json)")

# 2. Approve the 1 AUSD bond for the resolver
cast send 0xa9012a055bd4e0edff8ce09f960291c09d5322dc "approve(address,uint256)" $RESOLVER 1000000 --account flurbo-testnet --rpc-url https://testnet-rpc.monad.xyz

# 3. Assert (here: No)
cast send $RESOLVER "assertOutcome(uint8,uint8,bytes32,string)" $EVENT 1 $HASH "$SOURCE" --account flurbo-testnet --rpc-url https://testnet-rpc.monad.xyz

# 4. After the challenge period, if nobody disputed
cast send $RESOLVER "finalize(uint8)" $EVENT --account flurbo-testnet --rpc-url https://testnet-rpc.monad.xyz

# 5. Collect the 1 AUSD bond credit
cast send $RESOLVER "withdrawBond()" --account flurbo-testnet --rpc-url https://testnet-rpc.monad.xyz

The evidence URI must start with https:// or ipfs:// (8 to 512 characters). Keep evidence.json: its hash is what anyone can check against.

Dispute an assertion

cast send 0xa9012a055bd4e0edff8ce09f960291c09d5322dc "approve(address,uint256)" $RESOLVER 1000000 --account <wallet> --rpc-url https://testnet-rpc.monad.xyz
cast send $RESOLVER "dispute(uint8,uint8,bytes32,string)" $EVENT <other outcome> $HASH "$SOURCE" --account <wallet> --rpc-url https://testnet-rpc.monad.xyz

Reviewers then vote with vote(uint8 event, uint8 outcome, bytes32 rationaleHash, string rationaleURI) from their own wallets.

Where to look

  • https://flurbo.singu.online/healthz: bot status, cadence, next due time, registered pools.
  • Render logs: [settlement-monitor] lines per pool and [settlement-worker] lines with "mode":"registry", captures under sourceObservations.
  • Email: monitor alerts (Resend), the daily metrics digest, low-balance alerts.
  • Evidence: each assertion links its archived evidence at https://flurbo.singu.online/api/<namespace>/evidence/<hash>.
